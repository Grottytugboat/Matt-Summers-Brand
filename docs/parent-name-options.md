# Parent brand name options

Prepared 22 September 2026 for the shareable Brand Studio demonstration.

**Recommendation: Summers & Vale.** It connects naturally to Matt while sounding like a parent house with room to grow. It can sit above Nightbird, Benson’s and Mountain without becoming another competing product brand. Use **“A house of distinctive drinks”** as the descriptor and **“Distinctive brands. One point of view.”** as the opening line.

If Matt prefers a parent name independent of his surname, **Fieldmere House** is the strongest alternative. **Still & Field** is the strongest option with an immediate spirits association.

These are naming concepts, not claims of ownership, history, origin or availability. “Vale” is a creative landscape association, not a second founder. An established visual manner does not require invented establishment dates, coats of arms, heritage stories or manufacturing claims.

| Name | Why it belongs above the products | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Summers & Vale** | Personal connection, confident house structure, broad enough for future brands. | Makes Matt’s name part of the identity; confirm that is desirable. |
| **Fieldmere House** | Substantial, quiet and independent of any one product or founder. | Needs the drinks descriptor to establish the category. |
| **Still & Field** | Grounded and considered, with a clear spirits cue and attractive wordmark potential. | “Still” narrows the first impression towards distilled spirits. |
| **Rowe & Vale** | Short, balanced and adaptable across a premium portfolio. | An invented house name needs a real point of view to give it meaning. |
| **Harrow & Tide** | More expressive, with a strong contrast between grounded strength and movement. | Has a coastal impression; avoid invented location or sourcing stories. |
| **Halden House** | Architectural, restrained and easy to place above distinct product identities. | Halden is a Norwegian place name; avoid suggesting Norwegian origin. |

## Preliminary screening

This was a public-web screening pass, not a trade mark search or clearance opinion. Exact names, ampersand/“and” variants where relevant, and combinations with drinks, spirits, wine and Australia were searched. No obvious exact-name drinks operator surfaced for the six shortlisted names in the returned results. An absence from those results does not establish availability.

| Candidate | Representative searches | Observations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summers & Vale | `"Summers & Vale"`; `"Summers and Vale" Australia`; `"Summers & Vale" drinks` | No obvious exact-name operator surfaced. The separate words occur in the category: an Australian restaurant lists Possingham & Summers wine from McLaren Vale. [Observed menu](https://misspho.com.au/drink). |
| Fieldmere House | `"Fieldmere House"`; `"Fieldmere House" drinks wine spirits`; `"Fieldmere" drinks spirits wine` | No obvious exact-name drinks operator surfaced. | Still & Field | `"Still & Field" OR "Still and Field" drinks`; `"Still & Field" spirits`; `"Still and Field" Australia` | No obvious exact-name operator surfaced. Search results included generic uses of “still” and “field”; the constituent words remain broad. |

## Names removed during screening

- **North & Still:** too close to [North Stills](https://www.northstills.com/), an established distilling-equipment supplier operating in the same category ecosystem.
- **Crown & Field:** a drinks-sector near match surfaced as “Crown Field Wine Cellar – Segarcea” in an [existing hospitality wine list](https://casteltransilvania.ro/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Meniu-bauturi-Castel-Transilvania-TIPARIT-feb2019.pdf).
- **Summers Collective:** [The Summers Collective](https://thesummerscollective.com/) already publishes food, drinks and lifestyle content.
- **Vellmont:** existing premium commercial use by [Vellmont Watches](https://www.vellmontwatches.com/) makes it a weaker distinctive starting point.
- **Aldermark:** an active [Aldermark Limited](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/11393947) already operates in advertising and artistic creation.

## Before choosing the public name

The next naming decision is a preferred name and a reserve. Then check relevant business names, domains, social handles and similar trade marks before changing the public identity. IP Australia specifically recommends searching variations, misspellings and names that look or sound similar; an exact-word search alone is insufficient. [Australian Trade Mark Search guidance](https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks).

No names, domains or accounts have been registered. The Studio selector is a browser-only preview and does not rename the public website.
